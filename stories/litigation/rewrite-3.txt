After four years, we finally put the Ardent case behind us. I want to explain why the board chose to end it. They took us to federal court in Texas, saying our newest routers used their patented ways of passing data between wireless devices without asking. We have always said we did nothing wrong, and the paperwork says exactly that: we admit no liability.

Last year a jury ordered us to pay 310 million dollars. We appealed, but I told the board that a ruling could be a year or more away, and that we would spend about 25 million dollars a year on lawyers in the meantime. The agreement costs 200 million, half now and the rest in four quarterly payments, and in return both companies can use each other's patents.

We will book a 200 million charge this quarter, which takes about 31 cents off earnings per share. But it removes a cloud over our router sales, and we are dropping the appeal.
