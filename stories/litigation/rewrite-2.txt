Verita Systems announced on Monday the resolution of intellectual property litigation commenced by Ardent Networks four years ago. Pursuant to the agreement, Verita will remit USD 200 million to Ardent, half upon execution and the balance in four equal quarterly instalments. The parties also entered a cross-licence covering the patents in suit, which relate to methods for routing data among wireless devices.

Ardent had filed in the United States District Court in Texas, alleging that Verita's current router line incorporated its patented technology without authorisation. A jury returned a verdict for Ardent last year with damages of USD 310 million; Verita appealed and a determination was not anticipated for at least twelve months. Verita denied liability throughout, and the agreement contains no admission of liability.

The company will record a USD 200 million charge in the current quarter, reducing earnings per share by approximately USD 0.31, and stated that the arrangement eliminates uncertainty regarding router sales. The General Counsel advised that the Board concluded resolution to be in shareholders' interest, avoiding legal expenditure of roughly USD 25 million annually. Verita will withdraw its appeal.
