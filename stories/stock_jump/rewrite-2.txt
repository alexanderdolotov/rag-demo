Equity in Brightmart appreciated 12 percent during early Thursday trading following publication of quarterly results that exceeded consensus. Earnings per share of USD 1.84 compared with a consensus estimate of USD 1.52, on net revenue of USD 18.7 billion, an increase of 9 percent year over year.

Comparable-store sales growth of 5.1 percent represented the strongest outcome in two years, driven by grocery, home categories and click-and-collect fulfilment. Management cited trade-up behaviour among consumers and healthy inventories ahead of the seasonal peak, and increased full-year earnings guidance to USD 7.10 to 7.30 per share.

Sell-side analysts revised valuation targets upward: three firms raised price objectives by USD 10 to 15 per share and one upgraded its recommendation from neutral to buy, citing share gains from weaker competitors. The security closed at USD 96.40, its highest close since spring, on volume approximately four times the daily average. Some market participants observed that the advance had begun prior to the release and that a deceleration in consumer spending remained possible.
