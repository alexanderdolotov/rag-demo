Shares of Brightmart jumped 12 percent in early trading on Thursday after the retailer reported quarterly earnings that beat expectations. The company said earnings per share were $1.84, well above the $1.52 that analysts had forecast, on revenue of $18.7 billion, up 9 percent from a year ago.

Comparable store sales rose 5.1 percent, the best result in two years, led by groceries, home goods and strong online orders that customers collected in the store. Management said that shoppers were trading up in several categories, and that inventory levels were healthy heading into the holiday season. The company also raised its full-year outlook for earnings per share to a range of $7.10 to $7.30.

Analysts quickly raised their price targets. Three brokerages lifted their targets by between $10 and $15 a share, and one upgraded the stock from hold to buy, saying the retailer was taking market share from weaker rivals. The shares closed at $96.40, their highest level since last spring, after trading volume reached four times the daily average. Not everyone was convinced the rally would last. A few investors noted that the shares had already climbed in the weeks before the report.
