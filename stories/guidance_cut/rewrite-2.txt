Willowbrook Home Group revised its fiscal-year guidance downward on Friday, citing persistent softness in consumer demand extending into the second half. Net sales are now projected to contract by 4 to 6 percent versus prior guidance of flat to 2 percent growth, and adjusted earnings per share are projected at USD 2.10 to 2.30 versus USD 2.80 to 3.00 previously.

Comparable-store sales declined 5.8 percent in the latest quarter, with the weakest performance in furniture and kitchenware, housing-sensitive categories. Consumers purchased fewer big-ticket items and traded down to lower price points; store traffic decreased 7 percent. The Chief Executive Officer noted that customers are prioritising essentials and deferring larger purchases pending improved value, and that no recovery had been observed in the initial weeks of the current quarter.

The company will reduce planned capital expenditure by USD 120 million and close twelve underperforming locations by year end, affecting approximately 900 positions. The equity declined 9 percent in morning trading to a three-year low. The dividend was unchanged.
