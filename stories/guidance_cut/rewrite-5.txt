Willowbrook Home Group's share price dropped 9 percent to a three-year low on Friday after the retailer admitted that its year will be much weaker than planned.

The company now expects sales to fall 4 to 6 percent instead of staying flat or growing up to 2 percent, and adjusted profit of $2.10 to $2.30 per share instead of $2.80 to $3.00. Same-store sales fell 5.8 percent last quarter and store visits dropped 7 percent, hardest in furniture and kitchenware, as customers bought fewer large items and chose cheaper ones; the chief executive saw no recovery in early weeks of the new quarter.

Willowbrook will trim capital spending by $120 million and close twelve underperforming stores by year end, eliminating about 900 jobs. It left the dividend unchanged.
