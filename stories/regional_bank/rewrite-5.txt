Harbor Trust Bancorp earned 41 percent less in the second quarter, $118 million, because it set aside $85 million to cover loans that may not be repaid, most of them mortgages on office buildings.

The lender put aside $32 million a year earlier. Troubled loans have grown to 1.9 percent of its lending from 1.1 percent, centred on office towers in three large cities where more than a fifth of space is vacant. Written-off debts were $46 million, double the previous quarter, and the reserve stands at $780 million.

Deposits held at $41 billion and the capital ratio is 11.2 percent, well above requirements. The chief risk officer expects further losses for several quarters but at a manageable level. The company will pause growth in property loans, favour small business customers and keep its 34 cent dividend.
