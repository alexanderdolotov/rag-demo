Harbor Trust Bancorp released second-quarter results on Thursday, reporting that the provision for credit losses increased to USD 85 million from USD 32 million in the prior-year period owing to deteriorating performance in the commercial real estate loan portfolio. Net income declined 41 percent to USD 118 million, or USD 0.74 per diluted share.

Non-performing loans increased to 1.9 percent of total loans from 1.1 percent, concentrated in office properties in three major metropolitan markets where vacancy rates exceed 20 percent. Net charge-offs, representing balances written off as uncollectible, totalled USD 46 million, twice the prior quarter's level. The allowance for credit losses was USD 780 million, equivalent to 1.8 percent of loans.

Deposits were stable at USD 41 billion and the common equity tier 1 ratio remained at 11.2 percent, comfortably above regulatory minima. The Chief Risk Officer stated that all commercial property exposures above USD 10 million had been re-underwritten and that losses were expected to be manageable but to persist for several quarters. Management will refrain from growing the commercial real estate book this year and will prioritise small-business lending. The quarterly dividend of USD 0.34 per share was maintained.
