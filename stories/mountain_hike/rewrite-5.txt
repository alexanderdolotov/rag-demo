Four friends signed the old visitors' book in a stone refuge near the top of the mountain at midday, after a pre-dawn climb that their guide Tomas has repeated every September for thirty years.

The group, two sisters, a cousin and Tomas, set off in the dark with head torches and zigzagged through spruce forest before the trees gave way to a rocky stairway. The temperature dropped, a cloud briefly erased the view for ten minutes, and when it cleared the valley and the shining river lay below.

At noon, hungry and tired, they reached the low cabin with its grass roof and iron stove, where they warmed up with soup for an hour. Tomas wrote their four names in the register, and the party then moved on over the final ridge.
