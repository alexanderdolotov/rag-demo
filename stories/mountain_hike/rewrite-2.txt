A party of four undertook an ascent of the mountain via the northern route, departing before first light with head-mounted illumination. The group comprised two sisters, a cousin and an experienced family associate, Tomas, who had completed the ascent annually in September for three decades. The route commences in coniferous forest and ascends in switchbacks across loose scree and moist root systems.

At approximately 09:00 the tree line was reached and the route became a rocky stairway. Temperature fell and the atmosphere thinned; the party paused frequently for hydration and nutrition. A cloud layer passed over the slope, reducing visibility to nil for ten minutes before dispersing to reveal the valley and the river below.

At noon the group reached a stone refuge near the summit, a low structure with a turf roof, maintained by the mountain club. The interior contained a small stove, a bench and a visitors' register dating back decades. The party remained for one hour, consuming soup, before completing the final ridge section.
