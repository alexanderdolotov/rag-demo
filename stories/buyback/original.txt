Corvane Industries announced on Tuesday that its board of directors has authorized a share repurchase program of up to $5 billion over the next three years. The authorization replaces a $3 billion program that was completed in March, under which the company bought back 41 million shares at an average price of about $73.

The company said it expects to carry out the repurchases through open market purchases and accelerated share repurchase agreements, depending on market conditions, and that it will fund them from cash on hand and free cash flow. Corvane ended the last quarter with $9.4 billion in cash and marketable securities and has no debt maturing before 2029. The repurchase program has no expiration date and may be suspended or discontinued at any time.

In a statement, the chief financial officer said the program reflects the board's confidence in the business and its commitment to returning excess capital to shareholders. The quarterly dividend of $0.52 per share was left unchanged. At the current share price the new authorization would retire roughly 6 percent of the shares outstanding. Corvane shares rose 2 percent in after-hours trading.
