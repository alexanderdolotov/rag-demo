Corvane Industries stock gained 2 percent in late trading on Tuesday after the company said it would spend up to $5 billion over three years to buy its own shares, enough to retire about 6 percent of those in circulation.

The new plan, approved by directors, takes over from a $3 billion program that was finished in March and bought 41 million shares at an average of roughly $73. Corvane intends to purchase on the open market and through accelerated arrangements with banks, paying from $9.4 billion of cash and securities and from free cash flow. It has no borrowings due before 2029, and the plan may be stopped at any time.

The chief financial officer described it as a show of confidence and a way to return extra capital to shareholders. The quarterly dividend remains 52 cents a share.
