Corvane Industries said on Tuesday that its directors have approved a stock buyback worth up to $5 billion over the coming three years. It takes the place of a $3 billion plan that finished in March, during which the company purchased 41 million of its own shares at an average of about $73.

Management plans to buy on the open market and through accelerated deals with banks, depending on conditions, paying with cash it already holds and money generated by the business. At the end of last quarter Corvane held $9.4 billion in cash and securities and owes no debt before 2029. The plan has no end date and can be paused or cancelled at any time.

The finance chief said the plan shows the board's faith in the company and its commitment to handing surplus cash back to owners. The quarterly dividend of 52 cents a share was not changed. At today's price the new plan could retire about 6 percent of the shares in circulation. The stock gained 2 percent in late trading.
