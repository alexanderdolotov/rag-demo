The Board of Directors of Corvane Industries has approved a new equity repurchase authorisation of up to USD 5 billion with a three-year duration, superseding a USD 3 billion programme concluded in March, under which 41 million shares were acquired at a volume-weighted average price of approximately USD 73.

Repurchases may be executed via open-market transactions and accelerated repurchase agreements subject to prevailing conditions and will be financed from existing liquidity and free cash flow. Cash and marketable securities totalled USD 9.4 billion at the end of the most recent quarter and no debt matures before 2029. The authorisation carries no expiry and may be suspended or terminated at any time.

The Chief Financial Officer stated that the programme reflects Board confidence and a commitment to distribute surplus capital to shareholders. The quarterly dividend of USD 0.52 per share is unchanged. At current valuations the authorisation corresponds to retirement of approximately 6 percent of outstanding equity. The security rose 2 percent in extended trading.
