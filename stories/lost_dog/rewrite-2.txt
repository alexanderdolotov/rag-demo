Report of a missing canine, Sunday. The animal, a medium-sized brown dog with a white chest marking and a worn red collar, escaped from its residence through a gap in the perimeter fence while the owner, Mr. Okafor, was unloading groceries. The gate was found open and the animal absent.

During the following hours the dog traversed the surrounding residential area in rain, investigating street furniture and entrances and drinking from standing water. It passed the bakery, the closed school and a convenience store. Several residents observed the animal but did not intervene, presuming it to be accompanied or locally owned. On two occasions it rested beneath a bus shelter, exhibiting signs of fatigue and hunger.

In the late afternoon a minor, Mira, returning from a music lesson, located the animal sheltering behind a hedge. She read the telephone number on its identification tag, coaxed the dog into approaching and delivered it to her mother, who contacted the owner. Mr. Okafor arrived within fifteen minutes and the dog was reunited with him.
