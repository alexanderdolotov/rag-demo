I was heading home from my piano lesson on Sunday when I saw something brown shaking behind a hedge. It was a dog, medium-sized, with a white patch on its chest and a ragged red collar, and it was soaking wet. I could tell it was missing from somewhere. Nobody had stopped for it, though I think lots of people had seen it.

I crouched down and talked quietly until it came to me. There was a tag on its collar with a phone number, so I took it to my mum and she called. I held the collar the whole time, because I was scared it would run off again.

Fifteen minutes later a man in a coat came running up the road. He said his name was Mr. Okafor and that the dog had got out through a hole in the fence when he was bringing in the shopping. The dog jumped all over him. My mum gave him a towel for it. I handed over the dog feeling very grown up.
