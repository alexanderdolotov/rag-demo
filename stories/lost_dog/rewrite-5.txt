Mr. Okafor got his dog back on Sunday evening, thanks to a girl of about ten who had noticed it shaking behind a hedge and called the number on its tag.

The animal had been gone since early that day, when it escaped through a gap in the fence while its owner was unloading groceries. Soaked and hungry, it had spent hours roaming the rainy streets, past the bakery, the closed school and the corner shop; people saw it but assumed it was local, and it twice rested beneath a bus shelter looking up the road.

The girl, Mira, returning from a piano lesson, crouched and coaxed it out, read the tag and took it to her mother, who phoned the owner. He arrived within fifteen minutes, and the dog leapt at his legs.
