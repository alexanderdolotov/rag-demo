Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Appointment of Certain Officers. On Thursday Fairmont Foods announced that Daniel Reyes will cease to serve as Chief Executive Officer effective 31 December, following fourteen years of service. The Board has appointed Priya Nair, currently Chief Operating Officer, as Chief Executive Officer effective 1 January. Mr. Reyes, aged 63, will serve as senior adviser through the end of the following year to facilitate continuity.

Mr. Reyes joined the registrant in 2005 as head of the dairy division and was appointed to his current role in 2012. During his tenure annual revenues increased from USD 2.8 billion to USD 7.6 billion and the registrant entered frozen and plant-based categories through four acquisitions. Total shareholder return approximated 190 percent, versus 120 percent for the sector index.

Ms. Nair, aged 51, has served as Chief Operating Officer for five years and previously led European operations. The Board Chair stated that the succession process commenced two years ago and that the selection was unanimous. Ms. Nair will receive annual base compensation of USD 1.1 million and a long-term equity award. The registrant reaffirmed its fiscal-year outlook.
