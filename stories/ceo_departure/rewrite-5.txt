Fairmont Foods will have a new leader on January 1: Priya Nair, the company's operating chief, who was chosen unanimously by directors to take over from Daniel Reyes as he leaves the job after fourteen years.

Reyes, 63, leaves on December 31 but will advise the company through the end of next year. He built Fairmont from $2.8 billion in annual sales in 2012 to $7.6 billion, adding frozen meals and plant-based foods through four acquisitions; shareholders gained about 190 percent against 120 percent for peers.

Nair, 51, has been chief operating officer for five years and earlier ran Europe. She guided the merging of the last two acquisitions and a rebuild of the supply chain. The board chair said the handover had been planned for two years. Her pay will be a $1.1 million salary plus a long-term stock award, and the company reaffirmed its outlook for the year.
