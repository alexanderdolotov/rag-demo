Daniel Reyes came to me last spring and told me he thought it was time to go. After fourteen years running the company, he said he wanted to leave while it was in good shape, and he will hand over on December 31. I was sad, of course, but we had been planning for this for two years, so we knew exactly who we wanted.

That person is Priya Nair, our operating chief, who takes the top job on January 1. Every director backed her. She ran our European business before this, she has held her current post for five years, and she handled the merging of our last two acquisitions and the rebuilding of the supply chain. Daniel, who is 63, will stay on as an adviser until the end of next year.

When Daniel started in 2012, our sales were 2.8 billion dollars. Now they are 7.6 billion, and we sell frozen meals and plant-based products too, after four acquisitions. Investors are up about 190 percent over his time. Priya's pay will be 1.1 million dollars a year plus long-term stock, and our forecast for the year is unchanged.
