Fairmont Foods announced on Thursday that chief executive Daniel Reyes will retire on December 31 after fourteen years leading the company. The board of directors has named Priya Nair, currently the company's chief operating officer, to succeed him, effective January 1. Reyes, 63, will remain with the company as a senior adviser through the end of next year to support the transition.

Reyes joined Fairmont in 2005 as head of its dairy division and became chief executive in 2012. Under his leadership annual revenue grew from $2.8 billion to $7.6 billion, and the company expanded into frozen meals and plant-based products through four acquisitions. Shares have returned about 190 percent over his tenure, compared with 120 percent for the sector index.

Nair, 51, has been chief operating officer for five years and previously ran the company's European operations. She has overseen the integration of the last two acquisitions and the restructuring of the supply chain. The board chair said the succession plan had been in preparation for two years and that the board was unanimous in choosing her. Nair will receive an annual base salary of $1.1 million and a long-term equity grant. Fairmont reaffirmed its financial outlook for the year.
