Fairmont Foods said on Thursday that its top executive, Daniel Reyes, will step down on December 31 after running the company for fourteen years. Directors picked Priya Nair, now the operating chief, to take over as the new boss on January 1. Reyes, who is 63, will stay on as an adviser through the end of next year to help with the handover.

Reyes came to Fairmont in 2005 to run its dairy business and was promoted to the top job in 2012. During that time yearly sales rose from $2.8 billion to $7.6 billion, and the company moved into frozen meals and plant-based foods by buying four businesses. Investors have earned about 190 percent over his years in charge, against 120 percent for similar companies.

Nair, 51, has been the operating chief for five years and earlier led the company's European business. She managed the merging of the last two purchases and the overhaul of its supply chain. The chair of the board said the handover plan had been in the works for two years and that directors were united behind her. Nair will be paid a base salary of $1.1 million plus a long-term stock award. Fairmont stood by its forecast for the year.
