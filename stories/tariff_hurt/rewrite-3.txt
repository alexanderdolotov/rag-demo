I will be direct: this quarter was worse than we told you it would be, and the main reason is the new taxes on parts we bring in from abroad. Sales were 3.1 billion, flat on last year, but our profit fell to 214 million from 297.

Everything we build uses motors, circuit boards and compressors made in Asia, and since March almost all of them carry an extra 25 percent charge at the border. That pushed up what we pay for every unit, and our margin dropped to 27.4 percent from 30.5. That is three points, and nearly all of it is the extra charge.

We raised our prices by 4 percent in May, but the stores did not pass all of that on to shoppers and we sold fewer units in the final six weeks. We are signing up suppliers in Mexico and Vietnam, but that will not be done before spring, so you should assume the same hit to margin until then. We held the dividend at 40 cents.
