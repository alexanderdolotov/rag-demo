Halden Appliance Corporation announced second-quarter results below internal projections, attributing the shortfall primarily to newly imposed duties on foreign-sourced inputs. Net revenues were USD 3.1 billion, unchanged year over year; net income declined to USD 214 million from USD 297 million.

Management reported that the additional levies on imported components increased the cost base and compressed profitability. The company procures motors, printed circuit boards and compressors from Asian suppliers, nearly all of which became subject to an incremental 25 percent duty effective March. The gross profit ratio decreased to 27.4 percent from 30.5 percent, a reduction of approximately three percentage points ascribed almost entirely to the measure.

A 4 percent list-price increase was implemented in May; however, channel partners absorbed only part of the adjustment and unit shipments declined during the final six weeks of the period. Qualification of alternative suppliers in Mexico and Vietnam is expected to conclude by spring, and the margin headwind is projected to persist until then. The quarterly dividend was maintained at USD 0.40 per share.
