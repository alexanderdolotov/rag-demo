Item 1.05 Material Cybersecurity Incidents. On May 6 Lakemont Logistics identified a network intrusion involving unauthorised third-party access to a portion of its corporate environment. The registrant isolated affected systems upon detection. Its transportation scheduling and warehouse management applications were unavailable for approximately four days, resulting in delays to roughly 11 percent of shipments during the interval.

The registrant has retained an independent forensic firm and notified federal law enforcement. On the basis of the investigation to date there is no indication that payment card data or personnel records were accessed; the review remains ongoing. The registrant does not currently expect the event to impair performance under existing customer agreements.

Normal operations were restored on May 10. Credentials have been rotated enterprise-wide and monitoring has been enhanced. The registrant estimates the aggregate second-quarter impact, including remediation, professional fees and foregone revenue, at USD 15 million to USD 20 million, a substantial portion of which is expected to be recoverable under cyber insurance. Service credits will be extended to impacted customers.
