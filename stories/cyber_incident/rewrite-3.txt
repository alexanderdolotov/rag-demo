I was at my desk at the Lakemont depot on May 6 when every screen went dark. Someone had broken into our network, and the IT team pulled the plug on the affected systems before it could go any further. Without the scheduling system and the warehouse software we were doing everything on paper for four days, and roughly one parcel in nine went out late.

The company brought in outside specialists and called the federal police. As far as anyone can tell, nobody got at customers' payment cards or our own personnel files, though they are still checking. Management says our contracts are safe.

By May 10 we were back to normal. All of us had to choose new passwords, and there is more monitoring in place now. The bosses think the whole thing will cost fifteen to twenty million dollars this quarter, with insurance paying most. Our chief executive said sorry to the customers who waited and said they would get credits.
