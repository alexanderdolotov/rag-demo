I found out about the rate rise on my phone, standing in line at the bakery. The monetary authority had put its main rate up a quarter of a point again, the fifth time in a year, to 4.75 percent, to try to bring down rising prices. Eight of nine members voted for it.

I read the statement twice. Prices are 5.4 percent higher than last year, they said, which is far too fast, and jobs are so plentiful that wages are rising more than 5 percent, so they cannot ease off yet. The governor said they will keep going as long as they have to, depending on the numbers.

By the time I got home my mortgage company had already emailed me. My variable loan is going up, probably about $60 a month. My brother, who is saving for a house, says it is getting harder every time. The economists on the radio think there will be one more rise and then a pause.
