Northvale Devices lifted its reported profitability to 50.1 percent in the quarter, but about two points of that came from a one-time refund of duties on imports, not from the business itself.

The company, which makes handsets and sells subscriptions, reported revenue of $41.2 billion for the quarter ended June 27, a rise of 6 percent, with operating income of $12.6 billion. After a court overturned part of the charges the company had paid in earlier periods, it received the money back and booked it against the cost of what it sold. Without it, the figure would have been around 48.1 percent, against 46.3 percent a year ago.

The finance chief told investors the windfall will not repeat and is not in the forecast, which calls for 47 to 48 percent next quarter. A dividend of 26 cents per share was declared for holders on record August 11.
