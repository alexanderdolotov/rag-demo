Northvale Devices has released financial statements for the third fiscal quarter ended 27 June. Net revenues amounted to USD 41.2 billion, an increase of 6 percent year over year, attributable to demand for the latest handset generation and record services revenue. Operating income was USD 12.6 billion.

Margins improved as customs levies on imported goods were refunded. Following a judicial ruling that invalidated a portion of the levies assessed in prior periods, reimbursement was received and recognised as a reduction of cost of sales, contributing approximately two percentage points to the reported gross profit ratio of 50.1 percent (prior year: 46.3 percent). Excluding the reimbursement the ratio would have been approximately 48.1 percent, reflecting favourable product mix and lower input costs.

The Chief Financial Officer indicated that the reimbursement is non-recurring and is not incorporated in forward guidance, which anticipates a gross profit ratio of 47 to 48 percent for the fourth quarter. The Board declared a quarterly dividend of USD 0.26 per share, payable to shareholders of record on 11 August.
