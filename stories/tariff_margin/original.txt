Northvale Devices reported results for its fiscal third quarter ended June 27. Net sales were $41.2 billion, up 6 percent from a year earlier, led by strong demand for the company's newest handsets and a record quarter for services. Operating income rose to $12.6 billion.

Gross margin was 50.1 percent, compared with 46.3 percent in the same quarter last year. The result included a favorable impact of approximately two percentage points from tariff refunds, which the company received following the court decision that invalidated a portion of the tariffs it paid in prior periods. Excluding the refunds, gross margin was about 48.1 percent, still an improvement on the prior year on a better product mix and lower component costs.

The chief financial officer told analysts that the refunds are not expected to recur at this level. The company recorded the refund as a reduction to cost of sales in the quarter in which it was received and does not assume further amounts in its outlook. Looking ahead, management expects gross margin in the fourth quarter to be between 47 percent and 48 percent. The board declared a cash dividend of $0.26 per share, payable next month to shareholders of record as of the close of business on August 11.
