Northvale Devices posted quarterly revenue of $41.2 billion, up 6 percent. Reimbursed border taxes paid in earlier years added about two points to profitability, lifting the reported 50.1 percent figure from an underlying 48.1 percent. Management says the one-time reimbursement will not repeat, guides to 47 to 48 percent next quarter and declared a 26 cent dividend.
